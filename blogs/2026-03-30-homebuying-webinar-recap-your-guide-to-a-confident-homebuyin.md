<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Find The Right Mortgage Fit&nbsp;</h2>



<p>Every buyer’s situation is unique, and so are their loan options.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Conventional Loan (Non-Government&nbsp;Loan)&nbsp;</h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><em>Features and benefits</em>&nbsp;</h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Typical 5% down payment&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>No mortgage insurance with a 20% down payment&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Great for buyers with a favorable credit score&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<p></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">FHA Loan (Federal Housing&nbsp;Administration)&nbsp;</h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><em>Features and benefits</em>&nbsp;</h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Low 3.5% down payment&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Do not need to be a&nbsp;first-time&nbsp;buyer&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Lower credit score requirement than conventional loans&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">VA Loan (Department of Veterans Affairs)&nbsp;</h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><em>Features and benefits</em>&nbsp;</h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Low or no down payment&nbsp;required&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Active-duty, veteran military service members, and spouses of those who have served in the armed forces are eligible&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Lower credit score requirement than a&nbsp;conventional&nbsp;loan&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">USDA (Guaranteed Rural Development Loan)&nbsp;</h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><em>Features and benefits</em>&nbsp;</h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>No down payment&nbsp;required&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Property location requirements&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Some closing costs may be financed if supported by appraised value&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Collateral&nbsp;</strong></li>

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Capacity&nbsp;</strong>| Employment, Income, Debt-To-Income Ratio&nbsp;</li>

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Credit Score 101: What Matters Most&nbsp;</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11449" height="498" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Screenshot-2026-03-23-at-12.01.25-PM-1024x498.png" width="1024" /></figure>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Payment history and credit&nbsp;utilization&nbsp;make up 65% of your credit score&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Late payments&nbsp;can have a significant negative impact&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>A good general rule of thumb: Keep your balance at 30% or below the limit&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Example:</strong>&nbsp;If you have a credit card with a limit of $10,000, keep a balance of $3,000 or less&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

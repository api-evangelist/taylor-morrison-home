<p>The moment you start exploring homes, the excitement kicks in&#8230;and so does the choice between new construction and resale.&nbsp;Both options bring something to the table, but today’s market, lifestyle needs, and building standards make&nbsp;brand-new&nbsp;homes more appealing than ever.&nbsp;</p>



<p>To help make your search a little easier,&nbsp;here’s&nbsp;a friendly walk-through of what matters most when choosing a place to call home.&nbsp;Let’s&nbsp;dive in!&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Cost and Maintenance </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11407" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/new-v-resale-2-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Pros of Buying New </h3>



<p>For years, the&nbsp;perception&nbsp;has been that new homes cost more than older ones. But today’s market is rewriting that story.&nbsp;<a href="https://www.realtor.com/news/trends/new-home-price-cheaper-existing-data/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">According to recent Realtor.com® data,</a>&nbsp;new&nbsp;homes have been selling for less than resale homes for several months in a row,&nbsp;a pricing flip that&nbsp;hasn’t&nbsp;happened at this scale in at least 25 years.&nbsp;</p>



<p>That means buyers get all the long‑term benefits of new construction, often at a lower upfront cost than a comparable older home<em>.</em>&nbsp;</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><em>Finance Incentives</em> </h4>



<p>You’ll&nbsp;also find financing support designed to make the process smoother.&nbsp;<a href="https://www.taylormorrisonhf.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Taylor Morrison Home Funding, Inc.</a>&nbsp;continuously&nbsp;works to offer helpful incentives, rate&nbsp;locks, buydowns, and more&nbsp;so you can find a place that fits comfortably into your&nbsp;budget.*&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Cons of Buying Resale </h3>



<p>A resale home can come with a lower sticker price, but older systems and aging materials often mean&nbsp;costly updates, higher utility bills, outdated insulation, and big-ticket repairs that pop up when you least expect them.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Health </h2>



<p>Home is where the heart is. But it’s also where the rest of you lives, too. That’s why we’re committed to building homes that take care of all of you, with plenty of benefits to keep you and your home as healthy as possible. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11412" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/new-v-resale-5-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Pros of Buying New </h3>



<p>New homes are good for your health! According to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.iea.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The International Energy Agency,</a>&nbsp;new homes&nbsp;<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/3-ways-new-homes-healthier-than-older-francisca-alonso/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">“reduce symptoms of respiratory disease, reduce stress and infectious disease, and reduce risk of cancer, arthritis, depression, and symptoms of cardiovascular disease.”</a>&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Cons of Buying Resale </h3>



<p>It’s&nbsp;no secret that older homes can have negative health impacts. According to The Oregonian,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.oregonlive.com/homeideas/2017/02/potential_health_risks_of_remo.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">“Older homes are notorious for harboring numerous hazards like lead, asbestos, radon, [mold], and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC).”</a>&nbsp;</p>

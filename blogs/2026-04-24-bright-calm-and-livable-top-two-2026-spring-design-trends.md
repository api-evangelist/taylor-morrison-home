<p>Spring brings the opportunity to look at your home with fresh eyes. From exterior details that welcome guests to thoughtful updates inside, this season favors soft colors, warm finishes, and personal touches that feel intentional, never overdone. </p>



<p>Sound like a plan? Read on to discover the season’s latest design trends, courtesy of our National Director of Design and Model Experience, Lee Crowder. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Welcome Spring Through Your Front Porch  </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11483" height="576" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/GettyImages-2269740583-1024x576.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>The front porch is your home’s first impression. A quick update can get you, <em>and your lucky neighbors across the street</em>, in the mood for the warmer months. A few easy ways to refresh include: </p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>New welcome mats. Try out a braided one to add some interest with texture. </li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Pot flowers. A pop of color can immediately bring life and vibrancy to your front yard. </li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>A good power wash. Give your front porch the cleaning it needs to shine. </li>
</ul>



<p></p>



<p>If&nbsp;you’re&nbsp;in the mood for a bigger project with major impact, paint your front door in a&nbsp;classic&nbsp;spring hue to take your entrance to the next level.&nbsp;Our&nbsp;faves&nbsp;this season:&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Sherwin Williams Big Dipper (SW9645) &amp; Sherwin Williams Westhaven (SW9675)</h3>



<p>A moody, dark color that pairs well with pops of soft cream and bronze accents for a fresh take on 2026 spring design. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11478" height="1024" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Screenshot-2026-04-21-at-2.38.59-PM-953x1024.png" style="width: 629px; height: auto;" width="953" /></figure>



<p>Our next design trend: mixing antique and modern elements. Like they say, everything old is new again&#8230;<em>but with a twist.</em>&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Antique, Meet Modern </h2>



<p>Heritage-inspired design is having a moment. Vintage and traditional influences are showing up everywhere, from lighting to plumbing to hardware, and they’re landing comfortably in that sweet spot between classic and transitional. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="2026 spring design
" class="wp-image-11479" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/spring-blog-3-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>Warm finishes continue to lead the way this season. Burnished brass, champagne gold, and polished nickel are gaining momentum, especially when layered into kitchens and bathrooms for added depth.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p>Spring is the perfect time to mix&nbsp;antique and modern pieces&nbsp;rather than&nbsp;starting from scratch. A few easy ideas:&nbsp;</p>



<p>Pair an heirloom dining table with clean-lined, modern chairs.&nbsp;</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="2026 spring design" class="wp-image-11480" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/spring-blog-4-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>Style a traditional coffee table with contemporary lamps or accessories. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11481" height="1024" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/spring-blog-5-1024x1024.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>Upgrade hardware or fixtures for an easy and quick refresh.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11482" height="576" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sand-Dune-kitchen-2-1024x576.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>This approach keeps spaces feeling personal, collected, and current, while adding just enough spring energy to make your home feel refreshed and completely livable.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Thanks for Reading! </h2>



<p>As the season unfolds, spring design is less about bold changes and more about thoughtful updates.&nbsp;Whether&nbsp;it’s&nbsp;a refreshed front porch or a subtle interior update, these 2026&nbsp;trends are designed to impress—now and well beyond spring.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

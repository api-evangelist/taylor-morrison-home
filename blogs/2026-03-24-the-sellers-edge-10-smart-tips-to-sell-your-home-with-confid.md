<p>Getting ready to sell your home can feel like a big step, especially when it’s the key to unlocking the door to your next one. At Taylor Morrison, we want to help you feel empowered, not stressed or overwhelmed. With the right preparation and a few expert-backed strategies, you can position your home to attract buyers and confidently take the next step into a brand-new Taylor Morrison home. </p>



<p>Welcome to The Seller’s Edge, your guide to selling smarter.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. Declutter and Depersonalize </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11418" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/sellers-edge-2-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? <a href="https://mcallisterrealestate.com/real-estate-blog/minimalist-design-in-real-estate-does-less-sell-for-more/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Data has shown that clean and neutral homes tend to sell faster.</a>  </h3>



<p>Think of your home as a canvas. Putting away&nbsp;personal photos and excess items helps buyers imagine their own lives in&nbsp;the space.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p>Bonus: You’ll get a head start on packing for your move before you sell your home!</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. Boost Curb Appeal </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11419" height="682" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/sellers-edge-3-1024x682.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? A fresh coat of paint, new mulch, and a clean entryway can dramatically increase buyer interest and return on your investment. </h3>



<p>First impressions matter in the world of home shopping. Simple updates like trimming landscaping, adding potted plants, or refreshing your front door can make buyers excited before they even step inside.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Pressure Wash the Exterior </h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? Grime on your roof or siding can make your home look older than it is.  </h3>



<p>Refresh your home’s exterior&nbsp;for&nbsp;a&nbsp;cleaner, brighter, and better cared for&nbsp;look.&nbsp;A weekend pressure wash can make it shine.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. Consider a Pre-Listing Inspection </h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? Getting a home inspection before listing can help you avoid surprises and negotiate from a position of strength when trying to sell your home.</h3>



<p>A pre-listing inspection can give you clarity and control. Address&nbsp;potential issues upfront to&nbsp;keep&nbsp;your sale on track.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. Invest in Professional Photography </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="sell your home
" class="wp-image-11420" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/sellers-edge-4-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? <a href="https://visuallysold.com/blog/real-estate-photogr" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Listings with high-quality photos sell 32% faster.</a> Consider aerial shots and video walkthroughs to stand out online. </h3>



<p>A lot of buyers look online before touring in person. Professional photography can drive more interest to your home from the start.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">6. Neutralize Odors </h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? Buyers can detect odors from spoking or pets instantly.  </h3>



<p>Deep cleaning, fresh paint, or replacing carpets ensures your home smells as good as it looks. A fresh, neutral scent helps buyers focus on the space, not distractions.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">7. Fresh Paint </h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? Neutral tones create a calm and welcoming atmosphere from the moment home shoppers step inside.</h3>



<p>Use fresh paint in warm whites, soft grays,&nbsp;and&nbsp; beige&nbsp;tones. Bold colors may not match your&nbsp;prospective buyer.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">8. Staging Works </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="sell your home
" class="wp-image-11421" height="662" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/sellers-edge-5-1024x662.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? <a href="https://themortgagereports.com/18516/home-staging-mortgage-sale-craig-berry" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Professionally staged homes sell 73% faster.</a> Even virtual staging can help buyers visualize the space. </h3>



<p>Staging highlights your home’s best features and&nbsp;makes&nbsp;every&nbsp;room&nbsp;feel purposeful and inviting.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">9. Price it Right from the Start </h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? <a href="https://firstimpressionhomestaging.com/2021/01/why-stage-a-home-for-sale/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Homes priced to the market sell up to 88% faster, while overpriced homes often sit for weeks or months as buyers pass them by.</a> </h3>



<p>Overpricing may cause buyers to hesitate, making it harder to sell your home. The importance of a competitive, well-researched price cannot be overstated. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">10. When You’re Ready to Buy, Take Advantage of Finance Incentives </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11422" height="576" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/sellers-edge-6-1024x576.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Did you know? Finance incentives can make a bigger difference in your monthly payment than a price reduction.  </h3>

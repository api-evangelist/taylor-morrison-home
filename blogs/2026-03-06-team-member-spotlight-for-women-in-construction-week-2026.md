<ul class="wp-block-list">

</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Tess Hughes | Senior Permit Coordinator in&nbsp;Sarasota, FL&nbsp;</h2>



<p>Tess Hughes spent her formative years on construction sites with her dad.&nbsp;While her role reaches&nbsp;far and wide, her&nbsp;day-to-day&nbsp;includes:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Working with county vendors on permitting documents to&nbsp;maintain&nbsp;construction timelines&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Assisting&nbsp;superintendents in the field with permit questions&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Let’s&nbsp;hear from Tess herself!&nbsp;</h3>



<p>&#8220;My role challenges me in the best way and constantly motivates me to be more efficient and grow. As a permit coordinator, I get to learn so much about the building process in addition to engineering, county&nbsp;processes&nbsp;and relationships, and how all the departments in homebuilding come together to create that final product—that dream home.”&nbsp;</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11385" height="683" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/hj-co-denver-trailstone-fairmount-lot-5-community-03-1024x683.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p><a href="https://www.taylormorrison.com/co/denver/trailstone"><em>Trailstone in Arvada, CO</em></a></p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Ensuring homebuilding plans are executed, including:&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Timely procurement of permits&nbsp;</li>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Starting homes on time&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Assisting&nbsp;the build alongside area construction leadership and superintendents&nbsp;</li>

<p></p>



<p>Here’s&nbsp;what Rose has to say to women considering a construction career:&nbsp;</p>



<p>“For women interested in joining the construction or homebuilding industry, my biggest piece of advice is to stay curious and step confidently into opportunities, even if they feel a little outside your comfort zone. Our industry is incredibly broad, and there’s space for every skill set—whether that’s project management, field operations, design, estimating, trades, sales, IT,&nbsp;purchasing, or leadership.”&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Amber Reynolds | Division Cadence Manager in Las Vegas, NV</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-11392" height="682" src="https://blog.taylormorrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/hj-nv-las-vegas-ovation-at-mountain-falls-primrose-lot-5111-front-02-1024x682.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>For six years, Amber has held the vital role of Division Candence Manager,&nbsp;checking&nbsp;homes during the build process and ensuring her team stays on schedule. She&nbsp;walks&nbsp;the homes&nbsp;with superintendents to check that the correct options&nbsp;and safety measures&nbsp;are implemented.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Words of Encouragement from Amber&nbsp;</h3>



<p>Amber believes women truly are the future of construction.&nbsp;</p>



<p>“There’s&nbsp;roles in purchasing &#8230; field roles, even accounting roles in the homebuilding industry.&nbsp;It’s&nbsp;not just in the field and I would like to see women, more young women come into our industry. We are aging out, getting older, and we need more women in our industry.”&nbsp;</p>



<p> Her spirit and success are an inspiration for any women aspiring to join the field. </p>



<p>“It’s&nbsp;been rewarding to be in this industry and be able to deliver a beautiful home to a deserving family where you know memories are going to be made. I was part of that from the&nbsp;inception&nbsp;to pulling the permit, putting the roof on.”&nbsp;</p>

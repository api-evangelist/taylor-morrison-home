<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Frequently Asked Questions&nbsp;</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">How long does my reservation hold the home?&nbsp;</h3>



<p>Your reservation takes the home off the market for&nbsp;48 hours&nbsp;with a simple $100 refundable hold.&nbsp;And don’t worry, nothing is final, and you can change your mind later on in the process.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Can I make changes to my home&nbsp;selections&nbsp;after reserving?&nbsp;&nbsp;</h3>



<p>You can make changes if you are building a home from the ground up. However, if you selected a quick move-in home, it is pre-configured and move-in ready, so you cannot make any changes.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Do I need mortgage pre-approval&nbsp;in order to&nbsp;reserve a home?&nbsp;</h3>



<p>No. But you will need to get pre-approved through Taylor Morrison Home Funding, Inc. if you plan&nbsp;on moving&nbsp;forward with contracting on the home you reserved.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">How can I get notified about new homes becoming available to reserve in my area?&nbsp;</h3>



<p>If you find an area, community, or home you are interested in, sign up for more information on our website.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Do I need a real estate agent to reserve a home online?&nbsp;&nbsp;</h3>



<p>Whether you decide to use a real estate agent is up to you. Your Taylor Morrison sales representative can walk you through the entire process and answer any questions you may have.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Big news just dropped for anyone dreaming about life in an exciting master-planned community! <a href="https://www.taylormorrison.com/az/phoenix/aloravita-community-group" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Aloravita South</a>, <a href="https://www.taylormorrison.com/az/phoenix/artisan-at-asante-community-group" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Artisan at Asante</a>, and <a href="https://www.taylormorrison.com/az/phoenix/hawes-crossing-community-group" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Hawes Crossing</a> by Taylor Morrison just earned spots on the national <a href="https://jbrec.com/insights/top-50-master-planned-communities-2025/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Top 50 Master-Planned Communities list for 2025.</a> </p>



<p>The recognition comes from <a href="https://jbrec.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">John Burns Research and Consulting (JBREC)</a>, a trusted source for housing insights, and highlights communities where great design, meaningful amenities, and a true sense of place come together beautifully. </p>



<p>So, why did these&nbsp;three&nbsp;communities make the list? Read on&nbsp;for the details!&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Relentless Focus on Lifestyle </h2>



<p>JBREC research highlights the features most buyers gravitate toward: quality amenities, access to great schools, and thoughtful product choices that support different life stages.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Honorable Mention: Outdoor Spaces </h3>



<p>Outdoor spaces are especially important. Parks, playgrounds, gardens, and trails help create a nature-inspired, connected environment from the first day you move in.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
